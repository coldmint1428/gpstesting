The night we demo: NDP 2025, NE4 rehearsal

The demo uses real data instead of made-up groups. The times come from the NE4 Adjusted Show Matrix (17 Jul), and the routes come from the NDP 25 map, the same sources as our 1,000-phone stress test. The demo plan is a 95-minute slice of that night, from Parade & Ceremony to the contingents marching off, with 13 groups. T+0 is 17:54:50 show time. When the planner starts the event with T+0 = Now, Contingents 1 and 2 are already marching, Contingent 3 steps off 30 seconds later, and the Red Lions salute at T+2:55. All of that happens inside the 2-minute live segment.

A planner sets up an event in four steps. Every step validates as you type. For privacy, people are added only by their exact email: there's no search that shows other users' addresses.

SOS and attendance

On screen
  • Member window (the Contingent 3 cadet): SOS, note "Feeling faint, heat", Send
  • Phone (Contingent 3's IC): the SOS push and banner; laptop (root): banner and red pin on the map
  • Root taps "On my way" (as Medical Team 1 would); cadet sees "On the way: …"; then "I'm safe"
  • Phone (IC): Attendance by hand: tick the cadet present; the cadet's screen says "You are marked present"
  • Switch to QR mode: the code changes every 5 s
  • Cadet scans it (laptop webcam or a teammate's phone): "You are marked present"
Say

Heat injuries are a real risk on a parade evening. A cadet in Contingent 3 feels faint and presses SOS. It carries their location and an optional note, and everyone in the event gets a push and a pin on the map. The medical team taps "On my way", so the cadet knows help is coming, and the SOS closes when they're safe.

Before stepping off, ICs take attendance at the casing location. For a small group they tick names by hand. For a contingent of thirty, QR is faster: everyone scans the IC's screen. The code changes every five seconds, so a screenshot shared in a chat group won't check anyone in.

Will it fit in 12 minutes?

Yes, with room to spare, if the edit is tight. The narration is about 920 words, which takes roughly 6:30 at a normal pace (140 words a minute), and every segment's words fit inside its slot. The other five minutes are for showing the features on screen. Most overruns come from waiting on screen, not from talking.

Keep it inside the slot

  • Record each segment as its own clip, then time it. Anything over its slot gets cut in the edit, not re-explained.
  • Cut every spinner, page load and pause. Speed up typing and the test run by 2 to 4 times.
  • Sign every account in before you record. Supabase sign-in can take several seconds.
  • In the live segment, keep the phone recording the whole time, and use only the moments that matter: Share, the dot appearing, "Your times changed", Got it.
  • Speak over the action. Don't stop the demo to talk.

If the cut runs long, drop in this order

  1. Join an event (30 s): drop it; say "people join with a code" during the wizard.
  2. Wizard (60 s → 30 s): show only the exact-email check and Confirm.
  3. Map (60 s → 35 s): skip drawing; show the drawn venue, the address and the padlock.
  4. Playback (45 s → 25 s): END, then a few seconds of replay.
  5. Phones (45 s → 25 s): the cadet's Now tab only.

Never cut the problem, the plan, the live segment, SOS and attendance, or the one-slide stack and the testing segment (the brief asks for the data store, the API and testing).

How to record it

The phone (USB debugging)

Android's screen recorder runs over adb. Each clip can be at most 3 minutes, so start a new one for each segment. Turn on "Show taps" so viewers can see your finger.

adb shell settings put system show_touches 1
adb shell screenrecord --size 1080x2340 --bit-rate 8000000 --time-limit 180 /sdcard/s7-live.mp4
adb pull /sdcard/s7-live.mp4 .
adb shell settings put system show_touches 0

Ctrl+C stops a recording early. adb lives in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Android\Sdk\platform-tools. Claude can start, stop and pull these clips for you while you present.

The laptop

  1. Set the display to 1920 × 1080 at 100 % scaling, and Chrome zoom to 100 %.
  2. Use two Chrome profiles: planner (root) and member, both signed in before you record.
  3. Record with Snipping Tool (Win + Shift + R, full screen) or Game Bar (Win + Alt + R, Chrome only).
  4. Hide the bookmarks bar and close other tabs. Turn on Do Not Disturb.

Voice and edit

  1. Record the narration per segment in Clipchamp, which comes with Windows. Read from this page.
  2. In the Live, SOS and attendance segments, place the phone clip beside the laptop clip.
  3. Speed up waits and the test run. Cut every loading spinner.
  4. Export at 1080p and check the length is under 12:00. Upload to YouTube as unlisted, and put the link on slide 1.
