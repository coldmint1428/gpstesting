The night we demo

Segment 2 creates a small event live: NDP Rehearsal 4, with groups Navy and Air Force. From segment 3 on, we cut to the prepared event "NDP 2025 NE4 Rehearsal", made fresh for the take by node tools/demo/seed-ndp-demo.mjs (dated a week ahead, so a rehearsal can go before it). Its plan is a 95-minute slice of the real NE4 show matrix (17 Jul) on the NDP 25 map, with 13 groups. Start it with T+0 = Now, and the contingents are already marching.

The venue is the real one too, 43 areas in all. The Padang is laid out as it is built for the parade: a Performance Floor (a Stage) with the big East Stand along Connaught Drive, a North Stand and a South Stand at the ends, and the St Andrew's Road side open. On the floor are the formation rows, from the front: the State Colours, the Guard of Honour, the Regimental Colours Party, the uniformed Contingents, the Youth Uniform Groups, the Social and Economic Groups, the Combined Band and the Centre Stage. Around the field are the casing location and 29 areas from the NDP 25 Main map (KML), each on its own coordinates. The 22 named posts are drawn as small squares: control stations (PCS, VCS, DCS), access control points ACP 1–6 as Entrances, Medical Posts 1–3 and the VVIP Medical Post, and QRT 1–2. The KML's seven small unnamed outlines are drawn as they are, and named by us: the War Memorial Holding Area, Casing Pens A–C, a Marshalling Point, a Water Point, and the Fullerton Assembly Area. tools/demo/ndp-kml-areas.mjs turns the KML into docs/demo/ndp25-areas.json.

Say

Hi, we're Group 11, and this is Planova. Think of an NDP rehearsal: dozens of contingents, one venue, and everyone has to be in the right place at the right moment. Planova lets you plan where every group goes, and on the day, see where they actually are. What you see depends on your role. The root sets up the event, planners build the plan, and everyone else is in a group, led by a Group Admin and an IC whose phone shares its location. It all runs on a Supabase database that also enforces those roles, with maps from OneMap. rui?

Build the timeline

On screen
  • Timeline: a new plan; Place on map for two groups
  • Add a frame at +5:00 and drag the badges
  • Import the NE4 show matrix CSV: 13 groups, 418 moments
  • Play at 60×, then 120×; Ctrl + wheel to zoom
  • Shift-click a stretch of frames, Delete, then Undo
Say

Now the plan itself. A plan is a row of frames, each one a moment counted from T+0, when the clock starts. In the first frame we place each group on the map, then add a frame five minutes in and drag them to the formation area, and the app works out the walk in between. A real rehearsal is hundreds of moments, so run sheets come in as a CSV. This is the actual NE4 show matrix: thirteen groups, four hundred and eighteen moments. And a group can walk, take a bus or follow a float, so how far it moves between moments is the planner's call. Press Play, and every contingent walks its route, at sixty or a hundred and twenty times speed. Zoom keeps your frame in view, you can delete a whole stretch at once, and one Undo brings it back.

  • Share my location → the set-up screens; lock the phone; the notification with Stop
Say

So what does an IC see on their phone? Members get the same, just without location sharing. The Now tab shows where their group should be: the route on a map, each stop in order, and a countdown to the next one. Corners they only walk through stay out of the list. If a moment passes and nobody's marked the group, it turns late. When they get there, the IC taps We're here. Once the event's live, the IC taps Share my location. The first time, the app walks them through their phone settings, then it keeps sharing even with the screen off. And when the IC gets close enough, the group's marked arrived on its own.

Will it fit in 12 minutes?

Yes, with 0:25 to spare. The narration is 1,287 words, about 8:53 at a relaxed 145 words a minute. The rest of each slot is for clicks, so the clicking happens while you talk. Every segment's lines fit inside its slot.

Keep it inside the slot

  • Talk over your clip while it plays, and keep to your lines. Cut, don't re-explain.
  • Still moments over 1.5 s are cut out of the recording already, so the clips have no waiting in them.
  • A voice-over longer than its clip is fine: the last frame holds. Far over the slot isn't.

If the cut runs long, trim in this order

  1. The planner's phone (20 s): drop it; it's in the phone shots of segment 10 too.
  2. Playback (55 s → 40 s): skip the trails and Follow.
  3. Groups and people (30 s → 15 s): one group, no People page.
  4. Members and ICs (70 s → 55 s): skip the set-up screens.

Never cut the intro, the timeline, the live run, SOS or testing. The intro and testing segments carry what the brief marks.

Before you hit record

Make it look real

On the website and on the phone, the recording should look like a person using Planova on a real rehearsal night, not a script clicking through it.

  • Move like a person. The cursor travels in a gentle curve and slows as it arrives, lands inside the button rather than dead centre, and pauses a beat before clicking. Typing has a natural, uneven rhythm. The recording script does this for you; when recording by hand, don't jump straight to buttons.
  • No long pauses. Every still moment longer than 1.5 s is cut from the video. While a page loads or the plan plays, talk over it rather than waiting.
  • Real situations, accurately. Use the real NE4 show matrix and its real times: Contingents 1 and 2 already marching at 17:55, Contingent 3 stepping off at T+0:30, the salute slipping a minute. Real names (P&C Contingent 3, Red Lions), real places (the casing location, the Padang), and messages a planner would actually send ("Red Lions running 1 min late. Hold salute positions.").
  • Show results, not just clicks. After each action, let the result be seen: the badge moving, the "Your times changed" alert on the phone, the SOS banner, the problem clearing. Only then move on.
  • One flow, in order. Each segment carries on from the last on the same event: the plan built in segments 4–5 is the one that goes live in segment 9 and is played back in segment 13.
  • The phone too. Do Not Disturb on with Planova allowed, so no other app's banner appears; taps shown as dots; the phone stays on the event, as an IC would hold it.

Accounts and devices

  • The phone with the APK built on 4 Nov, joined to the demo event and added to P&C Contingent 3; location on, notifications allowed.

  • Playback needs a run with real movement: segment 13 plays back the run the take itself just made.
